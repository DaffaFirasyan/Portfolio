# Surface brief: portfolio, single page

Mode: Experience. Visitors: recruiters (primary), hiring managers, freelance clients. No single action outranks the others.

Pinned by the owner (2026-11-11, third round): the departure board was attractive but "too busy for a website". Replace the design and the theme. Everything that read as busy goes: tiles everywhere, the yellow bands, several colours at once, many things moving at once. Calm everywhere with one signature moment that is remembered. Warm paper and ink, one small accent. Light and dark still follow the device. Still light (CSS and SVG only, no WebGL, JS well under 100 KB gzip, Lighthouse mobile 90+). All seven sections, EN/ID and every word of copy kept.

Chosen from the dealt hand: the design annual's plate section (it was the assigned card). Offered and declined: the daylight section, a technical data sheet, and trimming the board.

Unresolved: none blocking.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a printed plate section. A hairline grid carries everything: the title in the left four columns, the evidence in the right eight. Type does the work, one grotesque at one weight, large where it matters and quiet everywhere else. Refuses effects as decoration, a second typeface for display, boxes around content, and any colour that is not paper, ink or the single seal.

OWN-WORLD: Uncoated warm paper and a never-pure-black ink, in both themes (warm graphite and bone ink at night). Hairline rules at 1px, never boxes. One accent, a vermilion seal colour, spent only on the letterpress stamp, the current link and the primary action. Geist at weight 400 for display, body and captions alike; JetBrains Mono at 11px uppercase and wide-tracked only for plate captions and credit-row labels, never above a heading. No shadows, no blur, no gradients. Registration crosshairs mark the corners of the hero plate and of each project plate. Recognisable with the words removed: hairlines, crosshairs at plate corners, and a round stamp half off an image.

STORY: In five seconds the visitor sees a very large name set like a title page and watches a stamp press itself half onto his portrait. In ten they know who, what and that he is available. Engineers read AssetMind as a plate with a caption; clients see the village portal as the second plate; contact and CV stay in a quiet top bar at every scroll position.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A thin top bar with the short name left and, right, the section links, EN/ID and the CV. Below it a twelve-column title page: his name at 8 to 9rem across seven columns, the role line under it, and a credits row of hairline-divided cells (location, status). In the right five columns a plate: a hairline frame with registration crosshairs at its corners, the portrait inside on a faint tinted panel, and a circular letterpress stamp reading his name, role and city that sits half off the frame's lower-left corner. On a phone the plate drops under the name and the stamp stays half off.

FORM: Design annual plate section, assigned by the roll (seed:6cae86ae198862e3), chosen over the daylight section, the data sheet and the trimmed board.
